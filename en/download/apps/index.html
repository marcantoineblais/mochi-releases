Download Mochi

Install Mochi on the computer that holds your movies and shows, then open it from any device, in your browser or in the Mochi app.

ServerApps

The apps play almost every file as it is, in its original quality. They're optional: Mochi also works in any modern browser.

Windows app

For Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) and Windows 11, 64-bit. It plays almost every file in its original quality, and your keyboard's media keys control the music.

  • It installs for your account only, without administrator rights.
  • On Windows 10, the installer offers to get Microsoft Edge WebView2 if it's missing.
  • At first launch, pick your server from the list, or type its address, like 192.168.1.20.

Android

For phones and tablets with Android 8.0 or later.

Download for AndroidAPK · 24 MB

On a phone or tablet

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.
  3. Open Mochi and pick your server.

Android TV

For TVs with Android 8.0 or later, Google TV included. It's the same app as on phones and tablets.

Download for Android TVAPK · 24 MB

On the TV

  1. On the TV, install the free Downloader app from the Play Store.
  2. In the TV's Settings, allow Downloader to install unknown apps. On Google TV, it's under Apps, then Security & restrictions, then Unknown sources. On other Android TVs, it's under Device Preferences, then Security & restrictions.
  3. In Downloader, type mochiplayer.com/tv and install the app.
  4. Open Mochi from your apps and pick your server.

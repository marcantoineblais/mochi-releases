Download Mochi

Install Mochi on the computer that holds your movies and shows, then open it from any device, in your browser or in the Mochi app.

ServerApps

Windows

For Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit). Mochi starts automatically with your computer and includes everything it needs.

  • During setup, tick “Let other devices on this network reach Mochi” to use it from your phone or TV.
  • Advanced settings are in C:\ProgramData\Mochi\mochi.conf. After a change, restart Mochi from its icon in the taskbar.
  • Movies on a NAS or another computer? Make sure this computer can access the shared folder.

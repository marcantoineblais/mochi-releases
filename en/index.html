What Mochi has to offer

You install Mochi on the computer that holds your movies, shows and music. It organizes them and streams them to every screen, at home or away. Everyone you invite gets their own profile.

Your next movie is one click away

All your media is available from an easy-to-use interface.

The details on your media are added for you

All that's left is to pick your preferred options and start watching.

An intuitive player

Reach your playback settings easily while your movie stays front and centre. On compatible devices, HDR video and styled subtitles (ASS) play as they are. On the others, most videos still play without converting the picture.

To each their own

Every user gets a profile with their history, favourites and language.

Cast your media to the big screen

Your phone becomes the remote for Mochi on your TV or computer.

The films shown are Blender Studio open movies, shared under a Creative Commons BY licence.

And much more

  • Share with family and friends

    Give your family and friends access to your collection, wherever they live.

  • Pick up where you left off

    Start in the living room, finish in the bedroom. Your progress follows you from screen to screen.

  • Skip intros and credits

    Mochi finds the opening and end credits automatically, so you can skip them in one click.

  • Your movies in their original quality

    Mochi plays your files as they are whenever your device allows it, up to 4K HDR.

  • A library that stays up to date

    Files you add to your folders show up in Mochi on their own.

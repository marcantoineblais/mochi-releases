Apps and TVs

Watch in a browser or in the Mochi apps for Windows and Android, and use Mochi on your TV with its remote.

In a browser

Mochi works in any recent browser, with nothing to install: open the server’s address, like http://192.168.1.20:6624 at home. When a browser can’t play a file as it is, the server converts it on the fly.

The Mochi apps

The apps for Windows and Android show the same Mochi as the browser, with a player of their own. They play almost every file as it is, in its original quality, so the server rarely has to convert anything. Download them from the download page.

When an app opens for the first time, it asks to Connect to your Mochi server:

  • Servers found on your home network are listed under On this network. Pick yours.
  • Otherwise, type the Server address, the one that opens Mochi in a browser, like 192.168.1.20 or mochi.home:6624.
  • For a server on the internet, type its address, like mochi.example.com. The app connects to it with https.

To connect to another server later, choose Change server on the sign-in screen.

Since the apps show the server’s own pages, they get Mochi’s new features as soon as the server is updated.

Windows

The app installs for your account only, without administrator rights. On Windows 10, the installer offers to get Microsoft Edge WebView2 if it’s missing.

Your keyboard’s media keys control the music.

Android phones and tablets

  • Sign in on a TV by scanning its code: see People and profiles.
  • Music keeps playing with the screen off, with controls on the lock screen and in the notifications.
  • Resume from your car or headphones, in Settings under Playback, lets Play on your car or headphones pick up your queue, even when Mochi is closed.

Android TV and Google TV

The same app shows up with your TV’s other apps. Move between buttons with the remote’s arrows, and press the center button to choose.

In the player:

  • Press the center button to bring back the controls.
  • Press left or right to go back or forward 10 seconds.
  • Press Back to leave the player. On the home screen, press Back twice to leave Mochi.

To let phones and computers start playing on the TV while another app is open, turn on Open when another device plays here in Settings, under Playback. Android asks you to allow Mochi to display over other apps.

On a TV, Mochi is only for watching and listening. To set up a new server, add libraries, fix a title’s information or manage people, use Mochi on a phone or computer.

Watching

Find something to watch, use the player, and set your audio and subtitle preferences.

Home

The home screen brings your movies and shows together:

  • Featured: a big banner at the top.
  • Continue watching: what you started.
  • Next up: the next episode of the shows you follow.
  • Your libraries: a tile for each library.
  • New in each library: the latest additions. See all opens the whole list.

To change the order or hide a row, go to Settings, then Home. Back to the usual layout undoes your changes.

Find a title

  • Browse a library: sort it by title, recently added, release date or rating. Filters narrow it to a genre, to watched or unwatched titles, or to your favourites. Titles by letter jumps through a long list.
  • Search: type a title or a person’s name.

A title’s page

Choose Play, or Resume where you stopped, or Start over. The More menu holds:

  • Mark as watched and Mark as unwatched.
  • Add to favourites.
  • Version, audio, subtitles and quality, to choose before you press play.

A show’s page has a Season picker and Mark season as watched. Below, you’ll find the Cast & crew, the Chapters, the Extras and other titles In the same series.

The player

  • Back 10 seconds and Forward 10 seconds jump around a scene. The player also shows when the video Ends at.
  • Drag the timeline to seek. A picture of the video shows above it when the library has timeline previews.
  • Audio, subtitles and quality opens the track and quality choices. Automatic (best) plays the best quality allowed.
  • Skip intro and Skip credits appear on the parts Mochi found. At the end of an episode, Next episode counts down. Choose Watch credits to stay.

On a computer, the keyboard works too:

Key Action
Space or K Play or pause
← → Back or forward 10 seconds
↑ ↓ Volume
F Full screen
M Mute
Esc Exit full screen, or leave the player

Playback settings

In Settings, under Playback:

  • Preferred audio: Mochi picks this audio track when a video has it.
  • Subtitle language and Show subtitles: When needed shows them when the audio isn’t in your language and for foreign dialogue. You can also pick Always, Only for foreign dialogue or Never.
  • Subtitle size: saved on each device, so a TV across the room and a phone can each have their own.
  • Skip intros automatically jumps past theme songs without pressing anything.
  • Play the next episode starts the next one when the credits roll.

When a video won’t play

Mochi checks the file on the server and tells you what’s wrong: a missing file, a damaged part, a file only partly copied. If the problem is with the file, let the person who runs your Mochi server know. If it skipped a damaged part, it says which.

Mochi guide

Install Mochi, add your movies, shows and music, give everyone their own profile and watch on any screen.

  • Getting started

    Install Mochi, create your account and add your first library.

  • Libraries and files

    Organize your folders so Mochi recognizes every title, and fix the ones it gets wrong.

  • People and profiles

    Give your family and friends their own profile, choose what they see, and sign in on a TV with your phone.

  • Watching

    Find something to watch, use the player, and set your audio and subtitle preferences.

  • Music

    Browse your music, build a queue and playlists, and take your songs on the go.

  • Apps and TVs

    Watch in a browser or in the Mochi apps for Windows and Android, and use Mochi on your TV with its remote.

  • Casting and the remote

    Send what you're watching to another screen, move it between devices, and use your phone as the remote.

  • Watching outside your home

    Let family and friends watch from their own home, through a secure https address.

  • Running your server

    Keep an eye on your server, set up video conversion and information sources, update Mochi and keep your data safe.

  • Migrate from Jellyfin or Plex

    Bring over what everyone watched on Jellyfin or Plex, where they stopped and when each title was added.

Migrate from Jellyfin or Plex

Bring over what everyone watched on Jellyfin or Plex, where they stopped and when each title was added.

Mochi reads your Jellyfin or Plex server and adds each person’s watch data to their Mochi profile. Your files stay where they are, and nothing changes on the other server.

What comes over

For each person you choose:

  • The movies and episodes they watched, and how many times.
  • Where they stopped in the ones they started.
  • When they last watched each title.
  • Their favourites, including shows (Jellyfin only).

You can also bring over when each title was added, so Recently added follows the order your old server got your titles in.

Music isn’t part of the import.

Before you start

  1. Add your libraries to Mochi with the same folders as on your old server, and let the first scan finish. A title gets its watch data only once it’s in Mochi. The folders can have another path on the Mochi computer: Mochi recognizes titles by their file and folder names, and those from Jellyfin by their TMDB, IMDb, TVDB or AniDB ids too.
  2. Add each person to Mochi in Administration, under People.

Both imports are in Administration, under Server: pick your old server under Import from.

From Jellyfin

  1. Create an API key in Jellyfin: go to Dashboard, then API Keys, and add one. Mochi uses it for this import only and doesn’t keep it.
  2. In Mochi, under Import from, pick Jellyfin. The section is called Migrate from Jellyfin.
  3. Type the Jellyfin Server address, like http://192.168.1.10:8096, and the API key. Choose Continue.
  4. Under Who’s who, pick the Mochi person for each Jellyfin user, or Don’t import. People with the same name are paired for you.
  5. Use the added dates from Jellyfin is on, so Recently added keeps Jellyfin’s order. To keep the day Mochi found each title instead, turn it off.
  6. Choose Check what changes. Mochi counts what it will bring over for each person, and the titles it couldn’t find in your libraries.
  7. Choose Import. Nothing changes until then.

From Plex

On Plex, each person signs in with their own Plex account, from any device, so Mochi can read what they watched.

  1. In Mochi, under Import from, pick Plex. The section is called Migrate from Plex.
  2. Type the Plex Server address, like http://192.168.1.10:32400.
  3. Choose Add a person, and pick who they are under Person in Mochi. Add one row for each person whose data you want.
  4. Choose Get a code. That person goes to plex.tv/link, signs in to their Plex account and enters the code. The row then says Signed in.
  5. Use the added dates from Plex is on. It needs Server owner turned on in the row of the person who owns the Plex server, since Mochi reads the dates as that person, who sees every library. To keep the day Mochi found each title instead, turn it off.
  6. Choose Check what changes. Mochi counts what it will bring over for each person, and the titles it couldn’t find in your libraries.
  7. Choose Import. Nothing changes until then.

Plex Home profiles without a Plex account of their own can’t be imported.

After the import

  • Watched and favourite marks are kept from both servers. Anything watched in Mochi since then stays as it is.
  • Importing again later doesn’t count anything twice. If titles were missing, add their folders to Mochi and import again.

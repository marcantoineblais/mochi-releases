Watching outside your home

Let family and friends watch from their own home, through a secure https address.

At home, devices reach Mochi through your network. To watch from anywhere else, your server needs an address on the internet that starts with https://.

What you need

  • A domain name that points to your home’s internet address, like mochi.example.com. A dynamic DNS service works if your internet address changes.
  • A reverse proxy on your network that serves that name over https and passes the requests to Mochi on port 6624. Mochi itself speaks plain http.
  • Your router forwarding ports 80 and 443 to the reverse proxy.

Caddy is a simple reverse proxy that gets the https certificate for you. With Caddy on the same network, this is the whole configuration:

mochi.example.com {
	reverse_proxy 192.168.1.20:6624
}

Tell Mochi its address

Go to Administration, then Server, then General.

  1. Under Public address, type the https address, like https://mochi.example.com.
  2. If the reverse proxy runs on another machine or in a container, add its address (192.168.1.2) or its network (172.17.0.0/16) under Reverse proxies. Only these can tell Mochi a visitor’s address.

If a proxy that isn’t in the list forwards requests, Mochi shows its address there so you can add it.

Over https, Mochi tells browsers to keep using https for that name. Use the name only with https://.

Keep streams within your upload speed

Your home’s upload speed limits what you can send out. Under Playback in the Server tab, set the Quality limit outside your home below it. Viewers at home aren’t limited.

You can also set a Quality limit for one person in People.

Share the address

Family and friends open the https address in their browser, or type it in the app’s Server address. Then they sign in with the username and password you gave them.
